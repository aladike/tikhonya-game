# Assets and software credits

## Original project assets

- Block atlas: `public/atlas.png`, generated by `scripts/generate-atlas.py` using the project palette; original 16×16 textures, CC0 1.0. No commercial-game textures.
- Block character, particles, clouds, butterflies and island landmarks: original procedural artwork, CC0 1.0 (implementation code: MIT).
- Day score and music engine: original day and night 64-bar compositions, harmony, bass, melody, arpeggios, synthesis and percussion in `src/data/music.ts` and `src/engine/audio.ts`, CC0 1.0 composition and sounds (implementation code: MIT). No sampled or third-party music.
- Material, interface, creature and laughter effects: original sounds, CC0 1.0 (synthesis code: MIT).
- App icons: original block-island artwork generated by `scripts/generate-icons.py`, CC0 1.0. System fonts and platform emoji are rendered by the device; no font files or external services are loaded.
